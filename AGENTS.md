## Original request
Build and evolve Asan Kaam, a Pakistan-focused marketplace where people post local tasks and verified taskers bid to complete them. Use for product, UX, Flutter, marketplace, trust, wallet, and launch decisions specific to Asan Kaam. --- # Asan Kaam product context ## Product promise Asan Kaam makes everyday work easy to get done and flexible work easy to find in Pakistan. It is an open local-task marketplace, not only a catalog of fixed home services. People can post lawful, practical tasks such as errands, moving an item, delivery, cleaning, small repairs, queues, digital help, and household assistance. Nearby taskers submit offers; the poster compares them and selects one. Use clear, simple English with optional Urdu-friendly copy. Design first for budget Android devices, unreliable networks, cash familiarity, and city-by-city launch. ## Marketplace roles - **Poster:** Posts a task, reviews bids, selects a tasker, and pays the agreed task price directly to the tasker. Posting is free. - **Tasker:** Builds a verified profile, maintains a platform balance, bids on tasks, completes work, and earns the task price. - **Platform:** Supplies discovery, verification, controlled communication, records, reviews, support, and service-fee collection. ## Revenue model - Do not charge posters a standard booking or service fee. - Charge taskers a commission only for successfully completed work. Start with a modest, configurable rate such as 5–8%; do not hard-code the rate in the interface or business logic. - Taskers need a minimum prepaid platform balance before bidding. When selected, reserve the applicable commission; deduct it only after verified completion. Release the reservation on an approved cancellation or dispute outcome. - Posters may optionally pay to promote or mark a task urgent. Make this clearly optional and show the price before purchase. - Treat the product balance as a ledger/service-credit feature until a licensed Pakistan payment partner and compliance design are in place. Do not describe it as an independently issued regulated e-wallet or promise escrow. ## Trust and anti-bypass rules - Before a task is accepted, do not reveal personal phone numbers, email addresses, social handles, external links, or precise home address. - Scan messages and bid notes for Pakistani phone patterns (`03…`, `+92…`), email, URLs, social usernames, and number words used to evade filters. Warn first; escalate repeated attempts through temporary restrictions and review. - Avoid offering open media sharing before acceptance. If media is necessary, detect contact information in images where feasible and allow reporting. - After acceptance, disclose only what is needed to complete the job. Keep task status, evidence, support, ratings, dispute handling, and rebooking inside Asan Kaam so users have a reason not to leave the platform. - Never claim that bypassing can be eliminated completely. Explain the safety and support benefits of staying in-app. ## MVP scope Prioritize these flows: 1. Sign-up and basic role selection. 2. Task feed with city/location, category, budget, timing, and task status. 3. Task posting and task details. 4. Tasker bid submission and poster bid selection. 5. Controlled in-app messaging. 6. Tasker balance, commission reservation, and transparent transaction history using mock data until a payment partner is integrated. 7. Completion confirmation, rating, reporting, cancellation, and a minimal dispute route. 8. Tasker verification states: unverified, submitted, verified, restricted. Defer payment-provider integration, identity-provider integration, insurance, telephone masking, live tracking, and nationwide expansion until their technical, legal, and operational decisions are confirmed. ## UX principles - Lead with trust: verification state, ratings, work history, clear fees, task records, and report controls. - Keep the poster experience free and low-friction. - Give taskers exact information before a bid or fee reservation: budget, location area, time, category, expected commission, and required balance. - Use PKR formatting and Pakistan-relevant categories and cities. - Never place phone-number sharing or payment instructions in default UI copy. - Model task states explicitly: draft, open, bid received, assigned, in progress, completion requested, completed, cancelled, disputed. ## Launch strategy Launch one focused zone in one city, with verified supply before broad demand marketing. Measure task-post-to-bid rate, assignment rate, completion rate, repeat rate, cancellation/dispute rate, and off-platform-contact attempts. Expand categories and geography only when reliability is proven. ## Engineering guidance - Build modular Flutter screens and keep marketplace/business logic separate from UI. - Start with deterministic mock repositories and models; replace them with authenticated backend services later. - Keep commission rates, balance thresholds, categories, safety rules, and feature flags configurable. - Do not collect CNIC, payment credentials, or sensitive verification data in a prototype without an approved provider and privacy/compliance design.
